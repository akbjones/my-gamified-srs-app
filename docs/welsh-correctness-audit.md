# Welsh Deck Correctness Audit

**Scope:** `src/data/welsh/deck.json` (3,512 cards)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Method:** Read-only. Pattern scans + manual inspection of Welsh `target` vs English `english`.

---

## 1. The reported card

The user cited a sentence meaning roughly *"your ankle bone and your bark are jealous."* It is real:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| id | `cy-0656` (deck.json:8003) |
| target | `Mae dy ffêr a dy rhisgl yn cenfigennus.` |
| english | `Your ankle bone and Your bark are jealous.` |
| grammarNode | node-05 |

This is not a mistranslation — the Welsh and the English **say the same nonsensical thing**. `ffêr` = ankle, `rhisgl` = (tree) bark, `cenfigennus` = jealous. The card was generated by slotting random dictionary words into a grammar frame, so both languages are garbled in lockstep. (It also has Welsh grammar errors — see §4.)

---

## 2. Root cause

These cards were produced by a **template + random-vocabulary generator**, not by translating real sentences. A grammar-drill frame (e.g. *"[Name] [past-verb] [adj] [noun] and [noun] [place/time]."*) was filled with vocabulary drawn at random from the dictionary, with **no semantic/collocation check**. The English gloss was generated from the *same* random slots, so target and gloss agree with each other while both being meaningless.

Evidence — same skeleton, different random fills, all in node-10:

- `cy-0283` `Coginiodd Nerys talent troellog a penelin yn y swyddfa.` → "Nerys cooked winding talent and elbow in the office."
- `cy-0364` → "Gwilym cooked deceitful wasp and blanket in the office."
- `cy-0384` → "Elin cooked unexpected accountant and swan in the office."
- `cy-0651` `Coginiodd Angharad ffens cenfigennus a clun ddoe.` → "Angharad cooked jealous fence and hip yesterday."

Other frame families with the identical defect:

- `cy-1230…cy-1331` (nodes 10–11): *"[Name] must [verb] [noun] and [adj] [noun]."* e.g. `cy-1331` `Rhaid i Guto berwi denim a ogof troellog.` → "Guto must boil denim and winding cave."
- `cy-2259`, `cy-2286` (node-20): *"[gerund] [noun] and [adj] [noun] is important."* e.g. "Laughing roebuck and wise wind is important."
- `cy-1782` (node-15): question frame → "When does Mair sell the strategy and the ankle bone?"

**This is NOT primarily a homonym/word-sense problem.** `bark`→`rhisgl` (tree bark) is a correct dictionary lemma; it only looks wrong because the whole sentence is random. Homonym confusion is a *secondary* risk in this generator but is not the cause of the reported card.

---

## 3. Scope

Garbled cards are **not spread evenly** — they cluster in a handful of grammar-drill nodes (node-09, node-10, node-11, with a few in 05/12/13/15/20). The rest of the deck (idioms, travel phrases, heritage sentences) is largely fine and reads naturally.

**Confirmed garbled: ~42 cards found by sampling** (lower bound; detectors were keyword/name-based and miss frames with unlisted names/verbs or single mismatched objects). Realistic total: **~50–80 cards (≈1.5–2.3% of the deck)**.

Detection was structural:
- Name-initial action-verb salad ("[Name] cooked/broke/bought/washed/lost/built/got … and …"): ~35 flagged (excluding legitimate ones like `cy-0938` "went to the shop and bought some milk").
- "[Name] must [verb] … and …" family: 10 (`cy-1230–1232`, `cy-1303`, `cy-1326–1331`) — all garbled.
- "… is important." gerund frame: 2 (`cy-2259`, `cy-2286`).
- Copula / question frames: `cy-0656`, `cy-1782`.
- Missed by first passes, found on node re-scan: `cy-0860` "Siân built soft document and chef", `cy-0986` "Eluned lost content ash and headteacher", `cy-1103` "Efa was trying benefit and traditional theatre" — confirming the count is a floor.

Distribution of the 39-card confirmed core: node-10 ×19, node-09 ×7, node-11 ×7, node-20 ×2, others ×1 each.

---

## 4. Secondary defect: Welsh mutation errors in the same cards

The generated Welsh also skips mandatory initial-consonant mutations, so even the grammar is wrong:

- `cy-0656` `yn cenfigennus` → should be `yn **g**enfigennus` (soft mutation after predicative `yn`); `dy rhisgl` → `dy **r**isgl` (soft after `dy`).
- `cy-0651` `a clun` → `a **ch**lun` (aspirate after `a` = "and"); `ffens cenfigennus` → `ffens **g**enfigennus` (adj. after fem. noun).
- `cy-1230` `Rhaid i Gruffudd **gweld**` → `… **weld**`; `a artisiog` → `**ac** artisiog` (before vowel).
- `cy-0283` `a penelin` → `a **ph**enelin`.

This confirms mechanical slot-filling: real Welsh source text would carry the mutations.

---

## 5. Designed fix (not whack-a-mole)

**Do not** hand-patch the ~42 known ids one by one — that leaves the undetected 10–40 and the root generator untouched.

1. **Quarantine the generator output.** Identify every card whose `target`/`english` came from the random-slot templates. Because they cluster by `grammarNode` and share skeletons, enumerate the frame families (past-verb salad, "must" salad, "is important" salad, question salad) and pull the full candidate set programmatically rather than by keyword — regex the skeleton, not the vocabulary. Expect 50–80 cards.

2. **Full rewrite pass, human/LLM-in-the-loop, semantics-first.** For each quarantined card, replace with a genuine sentence that (a) is meaningful, (b) still exercises the same grammar node (tense/construction), and (c) reuses target vocabulary the learner is meant to see. Generate Welsh *first* as correct native text, then gloss to English — never the reverse slot-fill.

3. **Add a Welsh mutation + collocation linter to the content pipeline** so this can't recur:
   - Mutation checks after `yn`, `a`/`ac`, `dy`, `i`, feminine-noun adjectives, etc.
   - A semantic sanity gate (LLM judge or collocation list) that rejects adjective↔noun and verb↔object pairings that never co-occur (e.g. "jealous fence", "boil denim").
   - Require `a` vs `ac` correctness and forbid abstract adjectives on concrete inanimate nouns without a whitelist.

4. **Regression guard:** a test that greps the deck for the known garbled skeletons and fails CI if any survive, plus a spot-check sample per grammar node.

5. **Audio note:** each garbled card has an `audio` mp3 (e.g. `cy-1306.mp3` for `cy-0656`). After rewrite, the affected clips must be regenerated and both cache versions bumped (`audio-cache-vN` in vite.config.ts + `AUDIO_VERSION` in audioService.ts) per project convention.
